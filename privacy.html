Privacy policy

Clockwork · Last updated 13 October 2026

Who this applies to

Clockwork is a private tool used by one person, its owner. No one else can sign in to it, so the only data it handles is its owner's own.

Data accessed

Clockwork accesses data in its owner's Google account, limited to the permissions the owner grants through Google sign-in.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to carry out tasks for the owner. It may be processed by service providers the owner has chosen for that purpose. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account settings. Access stops immediately.
